# NotiFlow — Notification Mirror for Android

**Real-time notification mirroring between Android devices**

NotiFlow lets you see notifications from one Android device on another — instantly. Install on both devices, pair via QR code, and you're set. No accounts, no ads, no trackers.

---

## Features

- 🔔 Real-time notification mirroring
- ↩️ Quick replies from the receiver device (Telegram, WhatsApp, VK)
- 🔒 End-to-end AES-256 encryption
- 📷 QR code pairing — no accounts required
- 🕐 72-hour notification history with search and filters
- 📍 Geofences — pause mirroring at home or any location
- 🔕 Per-app filters and content hiding
- 🔋 Battery status and sender online indicator
- ✅ "Mark as read" — dismiss notification on sender from receiver
- 📵 Screen filter — don't mirror when sender screen is on
- 🟦 Home screen widget with Play/Pause, Messengers-only, Geofencing toggles + sender battery

---

## Use Cases

**Second phone without a SIM** — keep a Wi-Fi-only device running separate apps, never miss its notifications on your main phone.

**Tablet in your bag** — notifications appear on your phone so you don't have to dig out the tablet.

**Phone on charge in another room** — notifications follow you to whichever device is in your hand.

**Work + personal phone** — carry one, stay on top of both.

---

## Permissions

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Notifications | Read and mirror notifications |
| Internet | Sync between devices |
| Camera | QR code scanning for pairing |
| Location | Geofence feature |

All permissions are requested automatically on first launch.
